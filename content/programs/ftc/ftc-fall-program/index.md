## Our First FTC Season

This is Shrewsbury Robotics' first year running a _FIRST_ Tech Challenge program! We're starting with one team, **37630: Potential Energy**, made up of 9th-grade students at Shrewsbury High School. We hope to expand the program to include more teams in future years.

{{< columns >}}

### Team 37630: Potential Energy

Our students work together to design, build, and program a robot for this season's game. Along the way, they develop engineering skills, practice problem-solving, and learn to communicate and collaborate as a team.

* **Students:** 9th-grade Shrewsbury High School students.
* **Meetings:** Four times a week at **Shrewsbury High School**.

## This Season's Challenge

Each season brings a new game with a new set of tasks for the robot to complete. Potential Energy will test and refine its robot as students prepare to compete in their first FTC season.

Visit the [official FIRST Tech Challenge website](https://www.firstinspires.org/programs/ftc/) to explore the current game and competition resources.

For a general introduction to the program, see [About FIRST Tech Challenge]({{< relref "/programs/ftc/about-ftc" >}}).
