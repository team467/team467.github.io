# FTC Fall Program

## Team 37630: Potential Energy

This is Shrewsbury Robotics' first year running a _FIRST_ Tech Challenge program!

We're starting with one team, made up of 9th-grade students at Shrewsbury High School and plan to expand the program to include more teams in future years.

Each season brings a new game with a new set of tasks for the robot to complete. Potential Energy will test and refine its robot as students prepare to compete in their first FTC competition - BIOBUZZ.

---

## Welcome to the BIOBUZZ Season

In BIOBUZZ™ presented by RTX, 2 competing ALLIANCES of 2 teams each collect POLLEN and NECTAR,
LAUNCHING them into their HIVE and placing them into FLOWERS.
